Congestion classifier

Balanced logistic regression on all 16 features flags trips slower than km/h. It is tuned for recall: it catches about 90 % of real congestion and accepts more false alarms in return. Location and distance inputs are held at the dataset average because their coefficients are close to 0.

Expected travel time by departure hour

Expected minutes = Σt P(traffic = t | hour) × distance ÷ speed(t, weather). The probabilities come from 50,000 historical trips and the speeds from the regression model.
